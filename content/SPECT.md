Single Photon Emission Computed Tomography (SPE[[CT]]) is a technique where the individual photons that are emitted from a [[Radioactive Decay]].  This makes use of [[Gamma Cameras, Classical and Solid State|Gamma Cameras]] to detect the locations of the [[Radioisotopes]] given to the patient.  And then, as is done in conventional CT, reconstructs a 3D image of the location of the activity.
