# Types of Error
Intra-fraction errors are ones that occur during a treatment, this includes both motion that the patient cannot control for, e.g. digestive processes, breathing, and heart beats.  As well as the motion that they cannot control for e.g. moving >: (

Inter-fraction errors are errors that occur between treatments, weight loss, tumour shrinkage, skin swelling are all examples that are frequently seen and can have major effects on the treatment.

Each of these kinds of error can either by random, or systematic.  Random errors correct themselves (to an extent) other the course of an extended treatment, whereas systematic errors will stack.

# What is it?
Adaptive radiotherapy is the alteration of a treatment plan during treatment in order to adapt to the changing circumstance of the treatment.  The most frequent cause of this will be a patients contour changing during the course of treatment such that there is a change in the dose distribution.

## Reactive Adaptive
A patients contour has changed and we want to re-plan them, this required short notice time from clinicians, radiographers, and dosimetrists.  While sometimes necessary this can be administratively expensive, and constant planning like this will create issues.

## Scheduled
Some changes are expected, a patient on chemo-RT is very likely to have significant tumour shrinkage over the course of treatment.  Although this takes the same amount of resources as a reactive approach, it can be booked in advanced, making it far more efficient.

## Pro-active adaptive radiotherapy
Create multiple plans that are for differing situations, this is the most common for bladder patients, with a small, medium, and large plan.  This requires a radiographer on set selecting which of these plans is most appropriate on a given day.  Most often known as "plan of the day".  This increases the resources from planning, and may require more training for radiographer to ensure they are comfortable with making these decisions.

The need for this training was proven by RTTQA, who looked back on the information from former trials, with initial results suggesting that the incorrect plan was chosen in 1/4 of cases.


## Real-time adaptive
This is where the patients plan is created on each day of their treatment, this requires a linac with very good imaging quality as a typical [[CT#CBCT|CBCT]] will often not have sufficient quality, use of an [[MR-Linac]] may enable this due to its improved soft tissue resolution.  In most cases this is done on every fraction, but is also expensive, both monetarily and resources wise.  Currently the only patients who go through this are on [[Trials-UN 14-10-2026-3|Trials]].
