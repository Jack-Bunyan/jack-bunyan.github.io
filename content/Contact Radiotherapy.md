Contact radiotherapy is a low-energy [[Radiotherapy]] technique that operates at around 50kV, this is done using a kV tube which is inserted inside of the patient, leading to very high [[Dose|Doses]], very conformal treatments.  This is useful compared to external beam radiotherapy which must pass through healthy tissue to reach the tumour.


![[Papillion dose contour graph.png]]

[Monte-carlo simulated dose distribution of papillon beam in water](https://www.brachyjournal.com/article/S1538-4721(24)00090-4/fulltext).  Also shout out to this paper for having the typo "depht \[sic\] dose profiles" which I find funny (I'm aware of the hypocrisy).

Contact radiotherapy recently became a [NICE](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/HTG763) recommended treatment for [[Rectal Cancer|Rectal]] cancer, this means that while it is currently a specialist device with only a few adopters, it is likely to increase in usage as other departments decide to purchase them.  The [OPERA](https://www.thelancet.com/journals/langas/article/PIIS2468-1253(22)00392-2/abstract) trial confirmed that when compared to external beam radiotherapy, contact Bracytherapy has a superior 3-year preservation rate.  Though they did report that the acute side effects were higher in the contact group, with the exception of radiation dermatitis.

While papillon systems are recommended for use with rectal, [[Breast Cancer|Breast]], and [[Skin Cancer|Skin]] sites.  Locally papillon is only used for its rectal application; this is a result of the NICE guidance for it.  There have been phase II [trials](https://www.ctro.science/article/S2405-6308%2822%2900090-8/fulltext) analysing the use contact radiotherapy during surgery, which support the use of intra-operative surgery; but, these have not yet advanced to phase III trials.

At our centre, roughly 3 treatments are performed on each of the days which this system is ran up, unlike other systems, where dose creeps upwards with time, papillon output trends downwards in most cases.  The system also runs of a car battery, which can run flat, preventing treatments, and will do so without any warning, if the system is not charged overnight it will not turn on, and cannot run using mains power.
