Of the different kinds of [[Dose]] which are considered the [[Dose#Equivalent|Equivalent Dose]] is often used as an example advantage of larger particle radiotherapy methods.  With [[On Particle Interactions#Electrons|Protons]] having a radiation weighting factor of 5.  This is used because while all types of radiation has an affect on the tissues it comes into contact with the same dose will have a different effect dependant on the delivery mechanism.

The increase in the radiation weighting factor of these tissues is a result of these heavier particles dealing more damage to the cell structure, this decreases the resistance of these tissues to radiation, whether due to oxygenation status or cell cycle location.  

This decrease in the ability for tissues to be resistance to the radiation means that the clinical window for proton and carbon ion therapies is shrunk, increasing the importance of dose distribution control through planning—though the Bragg peak will aid with this.  This reduction in the impact of the clinical window means that the number of fractions used can be reduced without increase in acute side effects.
