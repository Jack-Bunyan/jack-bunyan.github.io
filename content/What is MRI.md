Magnetic Resonance Imaging is a non-ionising imaging modality which is used for both [[fMRI|functional]] and anatomical imaging of patients.  Unlike CT it does not weight tissues based on attenuation so provides strong soft tissue contrast.

[[Atomic Spin]] is used to produce oscillations in the magnetic field which can be detected by the system and produce images.  Spatial information is encoded into this signal using [[Gradient Fields|Spatially Varying Fields]] to produce [[K-Space]] maps.  The Atomic spins that are excited using the [[Radiofrequency Pulse]] cane be altered to introduce different kinds of [[MRI T1 T2|contrast]] into the image.
